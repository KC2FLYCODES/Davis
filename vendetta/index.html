Questions

Before you book.

Where do you detail?

Anywhere in Raleigh, Durham, Cary and Apex: your home, office, or wherever the vehicle is parked.

How do I book?

Send a booking request below with your package, vehicle and preferred date. We confirm availability and pricing within 24 hours. For anything urgent, call or text.

How is pricing set?

By service, vehicle size and condition. A compact car that's had regular care takes less time than a third-row SUV after a road trip, and the quote reflects that.

Do you work on fleets?

Yes. Volume pricing is available for dealerships, rentals and company vehicles. Choose "Fleet / Commercial" in the form.
